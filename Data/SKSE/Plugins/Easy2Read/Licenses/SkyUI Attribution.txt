Used under SkyUI's open asset permissions. SkyUI icons are not included.

Easy2Read modifications: removed the solid black background fill, rasterized
the decorative frame at 4x, and packed eight border slices into an embedded
alpha atlas. Original corner geometry and curves are preserved; frame color,
opacity, and scale are configurable.

Extraction source and regeneration instructions are included in Easy2Read's
source repository under assets/SkyUI and tools/generate-skyui-frame.py.
